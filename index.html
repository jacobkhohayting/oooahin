Selected Best Problems

  1. (2026 FLSAM HMMT Tryout) Let $ABCDEF$ be a regular hexagon. Suppose $P$ lies on side $CD$ such that $PABC$ and $PDEF$ have areas $20$ and $26$, respectively. Compute the area of $ABCDEF$.
  2. (2026 FLSAM HMMT Tryout) Quadratic polynomials $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ with real coefficients are such that the polynomials $P(x)$, $Q(x)$, and $P(x)+Q(x)$ achieve unique minimum values when $x$ is $20$, $26$, and $25$ respectively. Find the $x$ for which $P(x)-Q(x)$ is minimized.
  3. (2026 FLSAM HMMT Tryout) Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with orthocenter $H$ and circumcenter $O$. Suppose lines $AO$ and $AH$ intersect segment $BC$ at $X$ and $Y$, respectively, such that $BX = 3$, $XY = 5$, and $YC = 2$. Compute the area of $\triangle ABC$.
  4. (2026 FLSAM ARML Tryout) An arithmetic sequence $s_1,s_2,s_3,\dots$ of reals is such that $$\frac{s_1}{2}=\frac{s_{s_1}}{11}=\frac{s_{s_{s_1}}}{53}\neq 0.$$ Compute $s_{10}$.
  5. (2026 FLSAM HMMT Tryout) Ten apples of distinct sizes are randomly arranged in a circle. An alligator is first located at the smallest apple and eats it. Every second from there, the alligator looks at the two apples adjacent to him and eats the larger one, repeating until there is only one apple left, which he also eats. Compute the probability that the alligator eats the second largest apple before the largest apple.
  6. (2025 FLSAM PUMaC Tryout) Compute the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $$3\left\lfloor \dfrac{n}{3}\right\rfloor - 4\left\lfloor \dfrac{n}{4}\right\rfloor = 7\left\lfloor \dfrac{n}{7}\right\rfloor - 8\left\lfloor \dfrac{n}{8}\right\rfloor = 11\left\lfloor \dfrac{n}{11}\right\rfloor - 12\left\lfloor \dfrac{n}{12}\right\rfloor = 1.$$
  7. (2025 FLSAM FLOMO) Find the number of $5$-tuples $(F, L, S, A, M)$ of digits such that $F\cdot L$, $L \cdot S$, $S\cdot A$, and $A \cdot M$ all have units digit $6$.
  8. (2026 FLSAM HMMT Tryout) Jason places each of his $100$ marbles numbered $1$ to $100$ into one of two distinguishable jars so that each jar has at least one marble. Compute the number of ways he can do this so that the product of the numbers in each of the two jars are relatively prime to each other.
  9. (2026 FLSAM HMMT Tryout) Distinct reals $a$, $b$, and $c$ satisfy $$ \begin{align*} a+b &= \sqrt[3]{2a+2b-11}+4 \\ b+c &= \sqrt[3]{2b+2c-11}+4 \\ c+a &= \sqrt[3]{2c+2a-11}+4 \end{align*} $$ Compute $ab+bc+ca$.
  10. (2026 FLSAM HMMT Tryout) Ten identical pandas sit in a row of $14$ chairs such that each chair is occupied by at most one panda. Each panda rolls either two chairs to the left or two chairs to the right. Compute the number of initial positions of the pandas such that it is possible for the pandas to occupy distinct chairs at the end.
  11. (2026 FLSAM ARML Tryout) Let $\mathcal A=(1,1,1,1,2,2,2,3,3,4)$. For a permutation of $\mathcal A$, let its \textit{quintessentiality} be the number of entries of the permutation (before the final entry) that are at most the next entry. Let $m$ be the minimum possible quintessentiality of a permutation of $\mathcal A$. Find the number of permutations of $\mathcal A$ with quintessentiality $m$.
  12. (2026 FLSAM ARML Tryout) Let $ABC$ be a triangle with centroid $G$. The circle centered at $G$ passing through $A$ intersects segment $BC$ at $P$ and $Q$ such that $BP = 6$, $PQ = 10$, and $QC = 2$. Compute the area of $ABC$.
  13. (2026 FLSAM ARML Tryout) Find the number of ways to place $9$ standard six-sided dice onto a $3\times 3$ grid such that for every pair of orthogonally adjacent dice, their touching faces have the same number.
  14. (2026 FLSAM HMMT Tryout) Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB = 24$. Points $P$ and $Q$ lie on segments $CA$ and $CB$ such that $PA = AB = BQ$. Suppose $AQ$ and $BP$ intersect at $R$. Points $S$ and $T$ lie on the circumcircles of $ARP$ and $BRQ$ such that $AS \parallel CB$ and $BT \parallel CA$. Given that $SR = 16$ and $RT = 20$, compute the area of $ABC$.
