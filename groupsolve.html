GroupSolve

GroupSolve is a non-profit organization targeting growth in mathematics for young children. The philosophy behind the program is to constantly be thinking. Whether it be in class discussions or homework assignments, we value students, and we will ensure they are always on their feet, actively thinking of ideas. Our program is heavily different from other programs as we emphasize the thought process of students.

How it will work

Students may sign up through this link in order to stay on the mailing list for classes. You will receive a confirmation email and will be notified each time I make an announcement.

Every week, students will be meeting for an hour-long class session on a single topic. The topics will cycle between algebra, geometry, number theory, and combinatorics to maximize coverage among the four big subjects. The goal is to eventually prepare students for premier competitions such as MOEMS, Math Kangaroo, AMC, and MathCounts.

Prior to the week, besides the first week, students should do their homework. Note that homework is not based on completion. In fact, students are not expected to completely finish; rather, give their best attempt. I prefer students to set aside a few hours every week to work on the problem set and give their best effort. Material taught in classes is based on homework from previous weeks, and so it is crucial that students attempt the homework.

About the instructors

The instructor for this course is me, Jacob Khohayting. I am a former USA Junior Math Olympiad Honorable Mention awardee. I have also placed in the top ten within the nation at competitions run at CMU, Princeton, MIT, and Harvard.
